# COMMUNITY UPDATE VERIFICATION

New community posts should initially be:

pending

A post becomes:

live

after *5 unique eligible users verify it*.

Security requirements:

* Users cannot verify their own post.
* Users cannot verify twice.
* Client cannot directly change status.
* Verification count must be calculated securely.
* Database/backend controls status transition.
* Prevent obvious automated abuse/rate manipulation.

Analyze whether the 5-person rule needs improvements and implement a sensible production-grade design.

---

# LOCATION MODEL

Every user has:

home_village_id

Users can:

* View their home village.
* Temporarily switch to another village.
* Browse nearby villages.

But users should only be permitted to create:

* Community updates
* Services
* Marketplace listings

within their authorized home-village scope unless an administrator explicitly allows otherwise.
